Real-time glass, in five terms.

Iridescence — IZAX light splits into colour as it bends through our glass. The green / violet sheen you saw on hover.

Index of refraction — How much light bends when it enters IZAX material. Higher IOR, deeper bend.

Dispersion — Different colours bend by different amounts. That is why IZAX glass shows a rainbow edge.

Transmission — How much light passes through. Our smoked glass keeps it dark, premium, and readable.

Roughness — How smooth the surface is. Low roughness = sharp studio reflection like IZAX Agency.
